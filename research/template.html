Bottom strip: LTF trend (green bullish, red bearish, grey neutral)
▲ ▼ sweep trades: the first candle that reaches the HL line (live once the HL is confirmed in a bullish trend or a break validates it) must wick through it and close back above it. Entry at that close, stop under the lower of the sweep wick and the HL wick, target the HH the pullback came from (or the highest high since the HL). Shorts mirrored at the LH. Red box = stop, green box = target, label = result after 0.1% fees.
HL LH level lines from the HL (body low) or LH (body high) to the first touch: solid = swept (trade), dashed = not reached; ✕ = the touch candle closed through the line (no trade, level gone).
HH LH HL LL swing labels, placed on the swing candle. Faded = internal swing (made at a break, not a real pivot).
▭ entry blocks: the full high–low range of each HL (green) or LH (red) that becomes active, either when it is confirmed in a matching trend or when a break validates it. From then on the box runs until price trades back into it (solid edge) or a newer swing on that side replaces it (dashed edge, never touched).
OB order blocks: zones inside a swing where an impulse started. A strong candle leaves a gap (fair value gap of at least 1× ATR between the candles around it); the block is the full range of the lowest candle (highest for red) in the 12 candles before it. Only with the LTF trend: green blocks need a bullish trend when the gap completes, red ones bearish. Ends when price trades back in (solid), or when a new swing on that side confirms or the trend turns (dashed).
