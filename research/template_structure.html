BTC Market Structure

OKX BTC-USD index · candles · market structure, leg swings
Bias bar: trend of the selected swing set (green bullish, red bearish, grey neutral). Legs %: the live bias, as known at each close (no hindsight): solid green / red = a confirmed trend, faded green / red = a bullish / bearish MSS that its first MSB has not confirmed yet. The zones use this bias: shorts only in solid red (a confirmed downtrend), longs in green or faded green (a pending bullish MSS is enough). RSI 14: Wilder RSI of the candle closes (≥ 70 overbought, ≤ 30 oversold)
HH LH HL LL swing labels, placed on the swing candle. Each label compares the swing with the previous swing of the same type (HH / LH for highs, HL / LL for lows). Legs, live (default): everything as the live structure knew it at the right edge of the view, with no hindsight: the swings and labels of the last rebuild, the MSS / MSB lines of that candle and the zones known by then (scroll to move in time; fully right is now). Legs, hindsight: the final legs (legs under the minimum % removed with hindsight). The minimum leg follows volatility: 4 × the 30-day ATR in % of price, as of the swing the leg starts from (about 2–4.5 % depending on the period). Structural swings (they set the structure: the levels that get broken and the extremes of the legs) are highlighted with an arrow and their label; internal swings are small faded dots without a label (toggle "Internal swings"): on the 2-day set a swing made at a break (not a real pivot), on the leg sets a swing inside the range that broke no structure.
▭ Supply zones: the candle that made each 8-candle swing high, its full range (a candle taller than 1% only its upper wick: from the high down to the body high, at least 0.5% tall), only when price dropped very sharply away from it (within 6 candles a low at least 1.5 ATR(14) below the zone). A box is drawn from that candle and only for zones that became known live (swing high confirmed) while the live bias (no hindsight) is bearish and before they were touched; it ends at the first candle that trades back into it after price left it, at a higher high, when the bias stops being bearish, or when a newer leg high settles (zones only count for the current swing). Zones touched before they were known are not drawn. Zones at a LH (the top of a pullback of at least the minimum leg % after the leg low, below the previous leg high) also need the bearish bias, but touches before the swing high is confirmed do not count. Bright boxes running to the right edge are still untouched.
▭ Demand zones (green): only at a HL (a pullback in an uptrend), with a less sharp rise than supply (1.5 ATR and at least 1% within 4 candles), and not ended by a bearish MSS (only by a confirmed downtrend); otherwise the supply rules mirrored. The candle that made each 8-candle swing low (a candle taller than 1% only its lower wick: from the low up to the body low, at least 0.5% tall), only when price rose very sharply away from it (within 4 candles a high at least 2 ATR(14) above the candle). Drawn while the live bias is bullish and before price traded back into it; ended at the first candle that trades back into it after price left it, at a lower low, when the bias stops being bullish, or when a newer leg low settles. Zones at a HL (the bottom of a pullback below the previous leg high, above the previous leg low) also need the bias; touches before the swing low is confirmed do not count.
┄ MSS / MSB: dotted lines at the current structure levels of the selected set (MSB = the last structural extreme, MSS = the swing that turns the trend when closed through). A close through the MSS line shifts the structure (against the trend); a close through the MSB line continues it. The bias bar follows the selected swing set.
